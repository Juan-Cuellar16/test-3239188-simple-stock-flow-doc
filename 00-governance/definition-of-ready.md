# Definition of Ready

> Proposed readiness checklist for documentation tasks. Team-specific approval rules are [TODO].

## A task is ready when

- [ ] The desired outcome and target document are identified.
- [ ] Relevant data-model sections, decisions, and findings are known.
- [ ] Facts are distinguishable from [assumption] statements.
- [ ] Relevant contradictions and pending tasks are identified.
- [ ] Acceptance conditions are clear and reviewable.
- [ ] Dependencies on other documents are known.
- [ ] Missing source information can be recorded as an open question instead of invented.

## A document task is ready when

- [ ] Required path and filename are confirmed from the challenge README.
- [ ] Related documents are available for consistency review.
- [ ] The supplied data model is understood as the source for reconstructed system claims.
- [ ] Any required owner decision is available or explicitly left open.
